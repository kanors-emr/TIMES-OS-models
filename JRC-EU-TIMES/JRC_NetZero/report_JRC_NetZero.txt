  SAME            every check that could be made against the published GAMS result agrees. With the published matrix the
                  two are the same linear program, and have the same optimum whether or not the solver finds it.
  SAME OBJECTIVE  the optimum is the one published; no more was published to compare with.
  DIFFERENT       the model runs and something does not agree with the published result: a defect. DIFFERENCES above says where to look.
  UNSUPPORTED     the model uses TIMES features this implementation does not have yet; they are all listed above. Not a defect.
  NOT SOLVED      the model was built and the solver did not reach an optimum (time limit, or a failure of the solver). julia.log has the solver log.
  ERROR           a step failed. If it is the preprocessor or Julia, it is a defect; the message is above.
  INFEASIBLE      the model's data cannot be met: rows left with no column whose constant does not satisfy them
                  (0 = 2.685), on which TIMES in GAMS stops too (equation infeasible due to rhs value). Not a defect.
  SOLVED          the open chain solved the model; there was no published GAMS result to compare with (--jacobian writes the matrix
